# PP-OCRv6 small ASCII transfer research

This is a bounded, independent fictional-development experiment. It does not enable a model in the application. Training is optional research; the original recognizer remains an explicit selection candidate. Reserved Sawarabi final fonts are not opened in this stage. iOS code/settings/remotes remain untouched.

`protocol.json` fixes sources, counts, seeds, training/evaluation budget, teacher loss, acceptance diagnostic and candidate selection before the run. `data.py` builds balanced arbitrary ASCII/case/confusable controls plus CJK/mixed/clipped/multiline/noise/blank negatives. It excludes prior comparison, synthetic training/evaluation and main PDF research literals, including the parent-provided fresh physical-frame oracle, solely for deduplication. No school originals are read or used. Each positive line uses one shared font baseline; paired contrasts share font and rendering seed. Synthetic clipping occurs before source framing and records actual nonwhite ink loss.

All RGB samples differing from white255 define one bounding rectangle expanded by two original pixels. Every nonwhite sample **in the provided original crop** is retained; degraded/clipped original crops cannot recover already lost ink. No whitening, ink threshold, glyph-wise resizing, internal-gap repair or literal-dependent framing is used. Input uses the existing production `image_tensor.py`: BGR48, original-pixel-center bilinear+uint8 round, width320..960, normalized-zero padding. Corpus records preserve source dimensions/offset/baseline/valid width. CTC time count is the actual model output, not an assumed stride.

`model.py` loads the pinned official unadapted small safetensors locally, verifies its SHA, and retains all learned CNN/SVTR features. Blank and all95 printableASCII linear rows/biases are copied exactly from the official18,710 dictionary. New dictionary: blank0, U32..126 indices1..95 (space1), UNK96. UNK initialization is the mean of excluded rows/biases, not exact logit aggregation or preservation of confidence. The zero-initialized separate reject head starts at0.5. CNN weights and BN statistics are frozen; the327,000-parameter encoder trains at a small learning rate. Total prototype3,023,186parameters.

`train.py` separately measures the original full-vocabulary model and reduced **untrained** head before updates. Reduction can inflate conditional confidence and alter decisions, so it receives no trained-improvement credit. A frozen full-vocabulary head provides teacher probabilities; its copied class mass and exact sum of all excluded mass into UNK form the retention KL target. Negatives target UNK and a reject label; they never target lost complete text. At most12epochs, CPU2, batch32, evaluations4/8/12 only, training30minute and peakRSS4GiB limits. Budget expiration may stop a partial epoch; only predeclared completed checkpoints are eligible. Frozen source hashes and raw outputs preserve provenance.

`probes.py prepare` freezes separate exact-pixel script-alias and fully removed-prefix contradictions. `probes.py evaluate` runs original/reduced and all completed fixed checkpoints after selection. These controls are never training targets or selection metrics. Identical recognizer inputs with incompatible original-script/crop provenance cannot be resolved by a learned crop reject head; rejecting both also loses valid ASCII. Independent complete-source/script evidence remains necessary.

`report.py` performs saved-artifact diagnostics, per-font exactness and prospective continuation-gate checks without loading a model. `final-opening-gate.json` was separately registered before epoch4: a trained selected model must reach97% raw exact,90% correct accepted,zero wrong/negative accepted and95% exact per dev font before considering consumption of final fonts or portable exports. This gate is only research continuation, not production qualification. Full documents, literal headers, source/ink/cell ownership, unsupported inputs and at most three actual body corrections still require independent validation.

Research paths are explicit: assets/corpora/checkpoints under `/workspace/scratch/ascii-paddle-transfer-20261013-v1`, reports under `research-results/ocr-synthetic-training-20261013/ascii-paddle-transfer-v1`. Pinned base assets/runtime come from the earlier training receipts. Train fonts were previously consumed. Dev KiwiMaru/HinaMincho were confirmed unused by the parent; both are pinned Google fonts with OFL receipts. ZenMaru was previously consumed in other research and is downloaded but unused here. Sawarabi Mincho's prior source-only exposure remains disclosed; final fonts are not rendered/inferred for this run.

Completed inference cannot be overwritten or replayed as new held-out evidence. No deployable ONNX/CoreML package is produced unless continuation is warranted. Any later package must retain BGR48, the distinct97-class dictionary, reject semantics, CTC padding refusal and original crop offsets, using standard ops. Linux CoreML conversion alone would not qualify an iPhone.
