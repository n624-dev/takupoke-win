# Stable dense original-image development and source-only role replay

Consumed ShipporiMincho / MPLUSRounded1c development drawings: 17 classes,
680 slots and 2040 body values per positive, plus missing-day and printed Al_1
controls. Actual image-only PDF source samples are independently verified.
A private runtime copy has identical recorded binary hashes before/after the
native run. Conditions and fixed two-original-pixel ink margin were frozen.
Every nonwhite nonrule source pixel, original CTC output, confidence and position
is retained; no confidence threshold change, clipping or output text repair.

Ink framing completes 680 source cells for each positive but leaves 529 and 306
low-confidence atoms. All native conditions refuse formal adoption. Negatives
also encounter positive quality failures, so their refusals cannot be credited
as proving intended defect detection. Printed Al_1 becoming AI_1 is a known
recognition error even though other failures prevent formal adoption.

A subsequent zero-model-call replay fixes duplicate prefix/colon observations
and preserves role words inside independently validated complete explicit native
field lines. It also preserves corresponding mixed bare/colon parallel labels.
Both dense positives now have three role scopes in all 680 cells, but still
refuse with humanUnresolved. This closes one source-structure bug; it does not
qualify OCR accuracy. The native raw (about 108MiB) and replay raw (about 54MiB)
remain unchanged in owned local research storage, identified by hashes in the
receipts. This directory contains frozen metadata and summaries, not pixels,
models or truncated raw. No reserved final font, school source or iOS edit.
